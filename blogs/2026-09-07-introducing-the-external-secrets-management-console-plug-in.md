Red Hat recently released the initial version of the external secrets management console plug-in. It’s an extension of the Red Hat OpenShift web console, which lets you inspect any of the resources deployed by Red Hat’s portfolio of secrets management operators: cert-manager operator for Red Hat OpenShift, external secrets operator for Red Hat OpenShift, and secrets store CSI driver operator.
