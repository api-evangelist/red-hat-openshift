The arrival of a cryptographically relevant quantum computer, often referred to as Q-day, is moving from a distant theoretical mathematical challenge to an urgent timeline that security teams must plan for today. Bad actors are already engaging in harvest now, decrypt later activities. This means they are capturing and storing encrypted enterprise traffic, intellectual property, and data logs today with the intention of running them through quantum hardware as soon as it becomes available.At Red Hat, we believe taking this threat seriously means acting before the crisis arrives.
