Telecommunications service providers must transition to autonomous intelligent networks to manage 5G complexity and remain competitive. The article outlines a progression toward full autonomy using the TM Forum maturity model, describing a three-layer architecture supported by AI that enables closed-loop automation cycles to detect and resolve network issues without manual intervention.
